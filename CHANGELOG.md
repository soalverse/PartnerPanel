# Partner Panel changelog

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-07)

The very first version, and the start of something.

- **The panel:** a card you can move anywhere, showing your partner's name, level, zone, status (online, AFK, in combat, resting, dead) and health. When they are away, it shows where and when you last saw them.
- **Moods:** `/partner mood <text>` leaves a short message on your partner's card.
- **Linking:** `/partner link` while you are grouped sends your partner a popup to accept, and the link is remembered between sessions.
- **Demo mode:** `/partner demo` shows the card with a pretend partner, so you can try it on your own.
- **Commands:** `/partner show`, `hide`, `lock`, `unlock`, `reset` and `status`, plus `/partner` on its own for the full list.
