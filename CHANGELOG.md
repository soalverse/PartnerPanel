# Partner Panel changelog

## v0.4.0 (2026-11-16)

Pings you can actually see, and a scrapbook of the two of you.

- **Pings float over the spot:** a little glowing icon hangs over the exact place you pinged, with its name and the yards, so your partner can see it out in the world and not only on the map. Five new animated icons come with it: two feet, a trail to an X, a sparkle, an exclamation mark and a pointing hand.
- **Follow me over your head:** when your partner is close enough to see you, the sparkle sits right over your head.
- **A heart over your partner:** a glowing heart that follows them around. It is off until you tick it in `/pp options`.
- **Partner journal:** your firsts together and your partner history, with dates, across every character on your account. Type `/pp journal`, or use the button on the settings page.
- **Quieter chat:** pings, answers and new links arrive as small notes that fade on their own, and while a ping is live, your partner's card says where to look.
- **After updating,** close WoW completely and start it again (a `/reload` is not enough), so it can load the new pictures.

## v0.3.5 (2026-11-16)

A settings page, and a ping key you can actually find.

- **The ping key:** in v0.3.0 it was meant to sit in Keybindings, and on WoW Forever it never showed up there. Now you set it on Partner Panel's own settings page: click the button and press the key you want.
- **Settings:** Partner Panel has a page in Options, under AddOns, and `/pp options` takes you there. Show and lock the card, make it bigger or smaller, fade its background, pick a theme, and switch off Alt-click asking or the sounds.
- **Card size and background** are new, and saved for each character, like your theme.

## v0.3.0 (2026-11-16)

Two new ways to talk without typing.

- **Pings:** hold your ping key, point, and let go. Stand here, Come here, Follow me, Watch out and Grab this put a pin on your partner's map and an arrow on their screen, where you are standing. Follow me keeps moving with you for three minutes. Set the key in Key Bindings, under Partner Panel, or type `/pp ping`.
- **Should I keep this?:** hold Alt and click an item in your bags to ask your partner Equip, Sell, Keep or Bank, and their answer stays on the item's tooltip.
- **The health bar,** honestly this time: WoW Forever keeps health private during a fight, so the bar steps aside until it is over.

## v0.2.0 (2026-11-09)

Your cards no longer need a group to stay live.

- **Live updates through Battle.net:** if the two of you are Battle.net friends, your partner's zone, status, health and mood keep updating when you are not grouped, so one of you can be off in a raid with other people and the other can still see where they are. Your group is still used first whenever you are in one together.
- **Linking without a group:** Battle.net friends can now link with `/pp link First Last` from anywhere.
- **`/pp status`** now says which route your messages are taking, and whether Battle.net has found your partner.
- **The card** says *Offline, or out of reach* when it cannot hear from your partner, and its tooltip shows how you are connected.
- **A shorter way to type:** `/pp` now does everything `/partner` does, so linking is `/pp link` and a mood is `/pp mood be right back`. The longer names still work.
- **Themes:** `/pp theme storm` repaints the card, with eight to choose from. Coral is the one you already have, and it stays the default.
- **Your partner's class on the card,** on the icon, instead of the same hearthstone for everyone.
- **The health bar** turns amber at half and red at a quarter, so you can see they are in trouble without reading anything. It also keeps going during a fight now, wherever WoW Forever allows it, which is exactly when you want it. Where it does not, the bar steps aside as before.

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-07)

The very first version, and the start of something.

- **The panel:** a card you can move anywhere, showing your partner's name, level, zone, status (online, AFK, in combat, resting, dead) and health. When they are away, it shows where and when you last saw them.
- **Moods:** `/partner mood <text>` leaves a short message on your partner's card.
- **Linking:** `/partner link` while you are grouped sends your partner a popup to accept, and the link is remembered between sessions.
- **Demo mode:** `/partner demo` shows the card with a pretend partner, so you can try it on your own.
- **Commands:** `/partner show`, `hide`, `lock`, `unlock`, `reset` and `status`, plus `/partner` on its own for the full list.
